Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. For emergency water extraction in Broken Arrow, Broken Arrow Restoration brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Add our number, (616) 376-4354, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Laminate or wood flooring that feels spongy and pushes up water at the seams
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home

Why Water Extraction Is Not a DIY Job

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.
